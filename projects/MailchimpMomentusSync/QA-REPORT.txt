LIMITATION

The new 3.3.0 queue screen was smoke-tested with a sample CSV but was not used to
submit a live batch during QA. The same underlying 3.2.0 engine completed three
live imports during this work, including the queued Webinar and Launch files,
with OPE/CLI details and zero engine errors. The 3.3.0 launcher was additionally
validated through source checks, Windows PowerShell parsing, .NET compilation,
release packaging, CSV fixtures, and a real WinForms load-and-close test.
